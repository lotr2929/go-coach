## Asked
Boon wants subscribers to build a grade in Go Coach from problems, lessons and games, accurate over time; asked whether KataGo could measure the computer's levels and help make problems. Then: record the decisions and run more measuring games.

## Done
- Recorded the plan in `docs/decisions.md` (new section "Go Coach grade") and the first measurements under "Engine".
- Downloaded KataGo 1.15.3 (CPU version) and its human-style model into the session's scratch folder (not the repository), and wrote a small match program there that plays Go Coach's engine (engine.js, unchanged) against KataGo imitating a human rank. 9×9, Chinese-style area scoring, simple ko, komi as Go Coach uses it. KataGo was run with lighter search (8 visits, one symmetry) to be fast enough without a graphics card; its moves still come from the human-style model.
- First 56 games: Hard about 6 to 8 kyu, Medium about 15 to 20 kyu, Easy well below 20 kyu (figures in decisions.md). 110 more games were started for the uncertain pairings.
