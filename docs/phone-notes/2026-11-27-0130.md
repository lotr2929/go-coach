# 27 Nov 2026, 01:30 (cloud session): reading the fight

**Asked:** Boon wrote out an analysis of one position (weak White stones by liberties, Black's safety, best move A (E8) with White's answers read out, then the other options) and asked for Go Coach to analyse and explain the board that way.

**Changed (branch read-fight):**
- New reading.js: reads short tactics with the local rules only. For each lettered suggestion it reads the opponent's sensible answers (play on a liberty of a weak group, counter-atari, capture, or play elsewhere) and your best follow-up (capture, or save a group in atari), and counts the stones won.
- coaching.js: when anything on the board has two liberties or fewer and one suggestion wins stones by its own threat, the analysis message shows "Weak White stones", "Black's stones", "Best move", "Also good", "Weaker options" and "Answer: A (E8)." in place of the percentage list. Otherwise it is unchanged.
- Wording in docs/text/en/reading.md. index.html and sw.js load reading.js.
- test-reading.js: Boon's position gives his answer and his explanations; 150 random positions never give broken text. Also run by hand on 3,000 random positions: no errors, under 10 ms each.
- docs/decisions.md: the decision, under "The coach's voice".

**Decided:** the best move is the one that wins most against the opponent's best answer. A move only counts as an attack if what it wins comes from its own threat (D6 in atari could be taken after almost any move). The order of the letters (from the random games) is kept, so "Best move" may be B or C.

**Left:** it reads only two or three moves ahead (no ladders, nets or life and death). Positions with no stones short of liberties still get the old explanation. Not tried in a browser.

**Try it:** play a game in Coach mode (or press Analyse) and get stones into atari; the analysis message should read like Boon's example.
