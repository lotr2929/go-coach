# Coach explains why one move beats another

**Asked:** coach feedback felt general; it did not really explain why move A is better than move B.

**Changed:**
- New file `explain.js` (`GoExplain.contrast`): plays both moves out to the end thousands of times and reports, in plain English, (1) how many points better A finishes, (2) which groups live or die after each (with percentages), (3) which part of the board the difference is in, (4) the opponent's sharpest answer to B (capture, atari, cut or rescue).
- `search.js`: new `ownership()` using the fast playouts (about 10 times faster than the old ones on 19x19). `areaScore` now uses a small `ownerOf` helper (same result).
- `coaching.js`: the evidence arrives as a follow-up message "Why X beats Y" (a second or two later) after: your own weaker move in Coach mode, "compare A and B", and "why A?" (compared with the next suggestion).
- `index.html`, `sw.js` load `explain.js`. New test for the explanation.
- Test fix: last session's "hard level captures a stone in atari" test was flaky (it passed by luck). At 0.3 s Hard captured in only about 30% of runs, though most other picks were also local moves that trap the stone; at the real 3.5 s it trapped the stone 11 of 12 times. Replaced with a clear position (three White stones in atari that would escape): Hard captures 20 of 20 times.

**Decided:** the evidence is a separate follow-up message so the quick verdict still appears instantly. In Coach mode it runs while the computer is thinking, so the computer gets slightly less thinking time on that one move.

**Left:** the hint ("Try X") and the analysis ranking still use the old method. Not tried in a browser.

**Try it:** preview → Play → switch to Coach mode → play a weak move (e.g. on the edge early on) and wait a second for "Why X beats Y". Also press Analyse and ask "compare A and B" or "why B?".
