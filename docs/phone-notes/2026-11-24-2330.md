# More checked quotes from Boon's books

**Asked:** try seven more books as sources: Go on Go (Go Seigen's games, tr. Jim Z. Yu), Master Play: The Style of Go Seigen (Yuan Zhou, 2008), Go for Beginners (Kaoru Iwamoto, 1972), Go: The World's Most Fascinating Game (Nihon Ki-in, 1973, two copies), The Thirty-six Stratagems Applied to Go (Ma Xiaochun 9-dan, tr. Roy Schmidt) and The 36 Stratagems for Business.

**Added to the coach (each with its book), chosen at random half the time instead of the proverb:**
- Building beats attacking: the Nihon Ki-in, "Go is often called the 'art of harmony' ... a self-centered way of thinking often leads to self ruin." (alongside the attributed Go Seigen line)
- A stone played too deep: Iwamoto p. 105 on thickness ("stand steady as a rock, and black stones may crash in ruin against it").
- Catching a weak group: Iwamoto p. 104 ("the fastest way to build territory is to do so while attacking"); Ma Xiaochun's stratagem "Loot a burning house".
- A sacrifice: Ma Xiaochun's stratagem "Sacrifice plums for peaches".
- A weakness left: Sakata Eio, Go on Go p. 133 ("To make every move be the most efficient ... one or two spots would naturally become thin").

**Found, not used (for Boon's pages):** Go Seigen in Go on Go: "One cannot win a game just because he wants to. The outside world is always disturbing. If my mind is shaken by this disturbance, I would lose." Yuan Zhou (Master Play p. 3) summarising Go Seigen: "the essence of the game is fighting and playing go is a search for the truth. The primary concern is not winning, but understanding." Iwamoto p. 107: "It is only common sense to drive your enemy towards your thick positions and away from your thin ones." "Go is harmony" was not found in either Go Seigen book.
