## 11:25 — the device's own full screen

page is full screen, and leaves it when neither is; page.js calls it from its setBig. Leaving the device's full screen (back
gesture, Esc) also ends the app's full screen. Browsers that cannot (iPhone Safari) keep the app's own full screen.

the browser, and a page full screen on its own all switch the device's full screen on and off correctly; no script errors.
