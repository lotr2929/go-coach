# A new, cleaner look

**Asked:** Boon decided against the ±10-move buttons (scrolling the move tree is enough), then asked for a redesign: cleaner and more modern, like SmartGo or AI Sensei, which he likes, but not a copy.

**Changed (look only, no behaviour):**
- style.css: new colour tokens at the top. Light by default: warm off-white background, white cards with soft shadows, one deep-green accent, rounded pill-shaped tabs and chips, rounder chat bubbles and input. A matching dark version applies automatically when the device is in dark mode.
- Title: left-aligned, sans-serif, with a small black-and-white stone mark (drawn in CSS). Headings in dialogs are sans-serif too.
- Game buttons: one row on a laptop; an even grid of four columns on a phone.
- The bar under the board spans the full width on a phone, so Territory is no longer cut off.
- board.js: slightly lighter wood, fainter grain.
- index.html / manifest: the browser bar colour matches (light or dark).

**Checked:** 70 tests pass; looked at it in a headless browser on a laptop size and an iPhone size, light and dark, and the New game box. Not tried on a real phone.

**Follow-up (Boon):** square corners everywhere (stones and number badges stay round); no borders on boxes (chat, move tree, notes, chat input, tour box); smaller buttons with minimal padding (4px 8px), each as wide as its word, wrapping on a narrow phone.

**Second follow-up (Boon):** no white boxes (--surface is now the app background), light outlines on the chat, move tree, notes, chat input and dialogs; buttons, chips and quick replies have no border or fill and show as clickable text in italics with a dotted underline; highlighted ones are green and bold. Icon buttons (settings, flag, enlarge chat) stay plain.

**Third follow-up (Boon):** no panel borders at all (chat, move tree, notes, dialogs, tour box); the chat input has no border, only an italic placeholder; buttons, chips and quick replies are plain words; a highlighted one (New, Send, Start, a tab that is on, a chosen option) is bold, same colour. Only the clickable choices inside the coach's messages (welcome, lessons menu) are italic with a thin (1px) dotted underline; the number or tick before them is kept apart (`GC.choiceLabel` in util.js) and not underlined.

**Fourth follow-up (Boon):** the chat box has only a thin grey bottom line, with "Ask your coach…" in grey italics.

**Fifth follow-up (Boon):** the move tree stays hidden until the first move is played (on an empty board its start square looked like a stray mark).

**Sixth follow-up (Boon):** Undo and Redo removed from the game buttons (hidden in index.html; they still hold the state the triangles follow and serve the chat's "undo"). The arrows under the board are now plain triangles (CSS, `.tri`), used for undo/redo and for back/next in Kifu and exploring; their words are spoken labels only. The tour's separate Undo/Redo step is gone. Hover colour only with a mouse, so a tapped button does not stay green on a phone.

**After going live (Boon):** no tint behind the coach's messages; a tapped quick reply is no longer repeated in the chat as a green box (the coach's answer already says what happened); what you type still appears, as plain grey words on the right with no box.

**Welcome (Boon):** the welcome message now ends "Type Tour to start a tour of the app." ("tour" was already recognised in the chat; "hint" was not used because it asks for a move hint). Boon asked whether the tour starts by itself on first install: it does not at present.

**Left:** the reading pages (History, What is Go?) keep their paper look; icons for the buttons could come later if Boon wants.
