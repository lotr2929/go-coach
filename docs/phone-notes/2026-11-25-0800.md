# A new, cleaner look

**Asked:** Boon decided against the ±10-move buttons (scrolling the move tree is enough), then asked for a redesign: cleaner and more modern, like SmartGo or AI Sensei, which he likes, but not a copy.

**Changed (look only, no behaviour):**
- style.css: new colour tokens at the top. Light by default: warm off-white background, white cards with soft shadows, one deep-green accent, rounded pill-shaped tabs and chips, rounder chat bubbles and input. A matching dark version applies automatically when the device is in dark mode.
- Title: left-aligned, sans-serif, with a small black-and-white stone mark (drawn in CSS). Headings in dialogs are sans-serif too.
- Game buttons: one row on a laptop; an even grid of four columns on a phone.
- The bar under the board spans the full width on a phone, so Territory is no longer cut off.
- board.js: slightly lighter wood, fainter grain.
- index.html / manifest: the browser bar colour matches (light or dark).

**Checked:** 70 tests pass; looked at it in a headless browser on a laptop size and an iPhone size, light and dark, and the New game box. Not tried on a real phone.

**Left:** the reading pages (History, What is Go?) keep their paper look; icons for the buttons could come later if Boon wants.
