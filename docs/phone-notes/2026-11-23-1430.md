# Shapes on the board

**Asked:** recognise shapes and show them on the board with a border and a light transparent colour; include shapes already made and shapes that could be made.

**Changed:**
- New `shapes.js` (GoShapes, pure rules, tested in Node): finds ponnuki, tiger's mouth, bamboo joint, diagonal (good); one-point jump, knight's move, eye (neutral); empty triangle, dumpling (bad); cutting point, crosscut, false eye (weak points). Patterns are tried in all 8 rotations/reflections. Clutter rules: links already joined in one group are skipped; diagonals/jumps/tigers inside a bigger shape are folded in; an "empty triangle" around an eye counts as an eye; a "cut" inside an eye is not a cut. `possible()` lists shapes the side to move could make with one legal move (tiger's mouth, bamboo joint to aim for; empty triangle, dumpling to avoid), at most one per point, at most 3.
- New `shapesui.js`: the Shapes tab next to Territory. Board: numbered rounded boxes, light fill, green good / red bad / blue neutral / orange weak; possible shapes dashed with a dashed ring on the move. Chat: turning it on lists each shape in one line, then explains each kind once; after each move the coach names any shape that move made. Works on your game and on famous games; hidden in lessons, teaching and explore (`shapes: null` added to their board calls).
- `board.js`: `drawShapes`. `play.js`, `kifuui.js`, `learn.js`, `teachui.js`, `exploreui.js`: pass `shapes`. `index.html`, `sw.js`: button and scripts. Two tests.

**Checked:** `node test.js` passes (49). Phone-sized headless Chromium: board and chat as described, no errors. 19x19 after 120 moves: about 0.16 s to find shapes (once per position). Not tried on a real phone.

**Left / ideas:** tap a numbered box to explain just that shape; more shapes (net, ladder, hane at the head of two stones, table shape); a setting to hide neutral links if the board feels busy on 19x19.
