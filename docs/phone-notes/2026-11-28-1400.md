# 2026-11-28 14:00 — the Computer panel scrolls, and a tap on a move goes to it

**Asked:** make the Computer panel scrollable; tapping a move takes the game back to that move.

**Changed:**
- computer.js: each computer move is a button. A tap calls play.js jumpTo (as the move tree does) with the line up to that move.
  The list shows the whole line: moves taken back (Play.redo) stay, greyed, so a tap on one goes forward again. The current
  move is highlighted and kept in view. Only while the game is being played (not while scoring or after the end).
- style.css: the list scrolls on its own (never the page); a 10px overflow of the page upright fixed.
- Wording: computer.go-to (the tip on each move).

**Checked:** tests pass; headless Chrome upright and on its side: 14 moves scroll inside the panel, no page scrolling, tapping
move 6 goes back to it (later moves greyed), tapping move 12 goes forward again, no script errors. Not tried on a real phone.

## 14:15 — the line stays until a different move is played

**Asked:** after going back, be able to return; keep the moves to go back and forth; change the list only if a different move
is played.

**Changed (play.js):** `ahead()` gives the moves taken back, in order; `keepAhead(i)` keeps the rest of that line when the move
played is its next move. jumpTo keeps the whole line when going back or forward along it (before, going forward to a move in
the middle dropped the moves after it). A move by the player that matches the line keeps it, and the computer then plays its
same move again instead of thinking afresh (no resigning on a replay). A different move drops the line, as before. This also
applies to Redo, the move tree and Pass.

**Checked:** headless Chrome, 14-move game: back to move 4 and forward to move 10 keep all 7 computer moves; from move 4,
playing the same move again gives the computer's same reply and the list is unchanged; a different move cuts the list to 4.
No script errors. Not tried on a real phone. The line is not kept after closing the app (as before).
