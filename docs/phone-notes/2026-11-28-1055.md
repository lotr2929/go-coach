# 2026-11-28 10:55 — reading pages size their text to the board window

**Asked:** the text of "Why Play Go?" looked much larger than the chat on Boon's phone; he wanted it to suit each screen,
without becoming too small on the laptop.

**Changed (style.css):** the page viewer is a size container; .pageBody's text is clamp(.85rem, .69rem + .89cqi, 1.05rem),
so it follows the board window's width (about 14.5px on a phone, 16.8px on a laptop, measured in Chrome), and headings, lists,
cards and references are in em so they follow it. Settings > Text size still scales everything (rem). Browsers without
container units get .95rem.

**Try it:** open Why Play Go? on the phone and on the laptop.
