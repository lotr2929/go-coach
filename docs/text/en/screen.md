# The screen

The words built into the screen: buttons, their tips (".tip"), the Settings box, the New game box and the result box.
Labels ending in ".aria" are read aloud by screen readers; ".hint" is the grey text inside the chat box.

How to edit: change the words under a "## name" heading, but leave the heading itself as it is.
