# The computer's reasons

Short reasons the computer gives for its own move ("Computer plays D4: captures a stone…") and when it passes.
Several reasons are joined with the word under "and".

How to edit: change the words under a "## name" heading, but leave the heading itself as it is.
Words in curly brackets, such as {count}, are filled in by the app: keep them.

## captures-one
captures a stone

## captures-many
captures {count} stones

## rescues
rescues a group that was in atari

## ataris
puts an enemy group in atari

## and
and

## third-line
a 3rd-line point: a good balance of territory and safety

## fourth-line
a 4th-line point: good for influence over the board

## sensible
a sensible move that builds on the stones nearby

## reading-ahead
a move found by reading ahead

## nothing-left
nothing left to gain

## no-moves
no useful moves left
