# How to use the app

The tour that highlights each part of the screen in turn. The same words appear as a tip when the mouse rests on a button,
or a finger presses and holds it. Each part has a title (".title") and an explanation (".text").

How to edit: change the words under a "## name" heading, but leave the heading itself as it is.

## intro.title
Welcome to Go Coach

## intro.text
The app has two parts: the board, where you play, and the chat, where you chat with your coach. This short tour shows you how to use both. Tap Next (or anywhere) to go on; ✕ ends the tour.

## title.title
Go Coach

## title.text
The date under the title is the version you are using. If it looks old, open Settings (⚙) and tap Check for updates.

## flag.title
Flag

## flag.text
If something looks wrong, tap here and type a few words. The position and the last messages are sent to the developer, as long as usage logging is on in Settings.

## settings.title
Settings

## settings.text
Coordinates, liberties, tap twice to place, stone sound, how long the territory map may think, copying the game record, usage logging, and Check for updates.

## whoturn.title
Whose move

## whoturn.text
How many stones each side has on the board. Your colour is underlined.

## ahead.title
Who is ahead

## ahead.text
Each side’s chance of winning, from the computer reading the position. About 50 : 50 means the game is even. Shown in Coach mode only.

## prisoners.title
Prisoners

## prisoners.text
How many stones each side has captured.

## board.title
The board

## board.text
Tap a point to place your stone. With "Tap twice to place" on in Settings, the first tap shows where it will go and the second places it. With Shapes on, tap one of your stones inside a box to hear about its shape.

## arrows.title
Undo and Redo

## arrows.text
Take back your last move, or play it again. In a famous game they step back and forward through the record.

## shapes.title
Shapes

## shapes.text
Marks good shapes in green, bad shapes in red, neutral ones in blue and weak points in orange. Dashed boxes are shapes you could make next. Tap a stone in a box for an explanation. In your own game it works in Coach mode only.

## territory.title
Territory

## territory.text
Shades each empty point by who is likely to own it, worked out from many quick games played to the end. Bigger, stronger squares mean more certain. In your own game it works in Coach mode only.

## mode.title
Play mode or Coach mode

## mode.text
In Play mode the coach stays quiet until you ask. In Coach mode it suggests moves every turn and tells you how each of your moves went, with the evidence.

## pass.title
Pass

## pass.text
Skip your turn. When both players pass one after the other, the game ends and is counted.

## analyse.title
Analyse

## analyse.text
The coach marks its best ideas A to E on the board, with each one’s chance of winning. Then ask "why A?" or "compare A and B".

## resign.title
Resign

## resign.text
Give up this game.

## setup.title
Set up

## setup.text
Place and remove stones by hand, for example to copy a position from a book, then play on from there.

## save.title
Save

## save.text
Keep this game on this device. Press it again to save your latest moves over the same game. Find your saved games under New, on the Saved games tab.

## new.title
New game

## new.text
Start a new game. Choose the board size (9 × 9 is quick, 19 × 19 is the full game), your colour or "Play both sides" (you choose the moves for both colours, with the coach suggesting moves for the other side), the computer’s level and any handicap stones. The Saved games tab keeps the games you saved, and lets you export a game as a file or import one.

## chat.title
The chat

## chat.text
Here you chat with your coach. Each new message from the coach moves to the top, so you read one thing at a time. Earlier messages fold to one line above: scroll up to see them, and tap a line to read it in full. Words in italics are choices you can tap.

## chatsize.title
Enlarge the chat

## chatsize.text
Tap here to make the chat fill the screen, for longer explanations and easier reading.

## chatbig.title
The chat, enlarged

## chatbig.text
Now the chat fills the screen. Tap the same button again to go back to the board. You can switch like this at any time.

## ask.title
Ask the coach

## ask.text
Type here, in a few plain words. The main keywords: "hint" (a good move), "why?" (the idea behind it), "analyse", a point such as "D4", "what is ko?" (any Go term), "teach me" (lessons) and "help" (the welcome). Type "keywords" any time to see everything the coach understands.

## send.title
Send

## send.text
Sends your message. Pressing Enter does the same.

## finish.title
Three words to remember

## finish.text
Type "help" to see the welcome, "keywords" to see everything the coach understands, and "tour" to see this tour again. Enjoy your games!

## back
Back

## next
Next

## close
End the tour

## end-mouse
That was the tour. Rest the mouse on any button to see its explanation again. Type "help", "keywords" or "tour" at any time.

## end-touch
That was the tour. Press and hold any button to see its explanation again. Type "help", "keywords" or "tour" at any time.
