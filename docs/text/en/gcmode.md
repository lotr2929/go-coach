# Go Coach mode

The daily game (gcmode.js): clicking the title "Go Coach" turns it on or off. Today's lesson at your grade, a streak of days,
then more lessons at your grade. {days} is "1 day" or "5 days"; {n} is a number.

How to edit: change the words under a "## name" heading, but leave the heading itself as it is.

## toggle
Go Coach mode: tap to start today's lesson, tap again to stop

## day
{n} day

## days
{n} days

## streak-badge
🔥 {n}

## streak-about
Your streak: {days} in a row with a lesson solved in Go Coach mode.

## welcome
Welcome to Go Coach! Here is today's lesson, chosen for your grade. Solve it first time to build your Go Coach grade, and come back tomorrow for the next one.

## welcome-streak
Welcome back! Your streak: {days}. Here is today's lesson, chosen for your grade.

## done-today
You have already solved today's lesson (streak: {days}). Here is another at your level: it counts towards your grade too.

## streak-started
Today's lesson is done: your streak has begun. Come back tomorrow to keep it going.

## streak-up
Today's lesson is done: your streak is now {days}. See you tomorrow!

## all-done
You have solved every lesson at your grade for now. More are on the way. Tap the title Go Coach to leave.

## no-lessons
The lessons could not be loaded. Check your connection and try again.

## limit
That is your {n} free games for today. Subscribers play as many as they like (subscriptions are coming soon). Come back tomorrow for {n} more and keep your streak going.
