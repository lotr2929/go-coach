# Exploring a move

"Explore D4": the coach follows a move through a few replies on a copy of the board.
Words in curly brackets, such as {point}, are filled in by the app: keep them.

How to edit: change the words under a "## name" heading, but leave the heading itself as it is.

## passes
{player} passes.

## plays
{player} plays {point}.

## tail-first
Say "next" to see how the game might continue.

## tail-last
Say "next" for a reply, "another" for a different idea, tap a point to try your own, or "back to game".

## tail
Say "next" to carry on, or "back" to step back.

## move
Move {count}

## start
**Exploring {point}**
I will follow this move on a copy of the board, one step at a time. Your game is not touched. Say "next" for each step, "back" to step back, "another" to see a different reply, or tap a point to try your own idea. Say "back to game" when you are done.

## far-enough
That is far enough for one line. Say "back" to step back, "another" to change a reply, or "back to game".

## no-reply-yet
There is no reply to change yet. Say "next" first.

## no-other
I have no other sensible idea here. Say "back" to step back, or "back to game".

## different-reply
A different reply

## first-move
This is the first move. Say "next" to go on.

## back-to-move
**Back to move {count}**

## your-idea
Your idea

## status
Exploring {point}: step {count} of {total}

## status-empty
Exploring

## in-progress
I can explore moves while a game is in progress.

## finish-first
Finish what we are doing first: say "back to game", then ask again.

## back
Back to your game. Nothing on the board has changed.

## show-me-start
**Show me**
I will play the best move and the likely replies by myself on a copy of the board, one move every two seconds. Your game is not touched. Type anything or tap the board to stop me early.

## show-me-nothing
I find no useful move to show here.

## show-me-done
That is as far as I go on my own. Use ← Back and Next → under the board (or say "back" and "next") to replay the moves, "another" for a different reply, or tap a point to try your own idea. Say "back to game" when you are ready to return to your game.
