# Set up

What the coach says while you set up the board by hand (the Set up button under the board, or say "set up the board").
Words in curly brackets, such as {colour}, are filled in by the app: keep them.

How to edit: change the words under a "## name" heading, but leave the heading itself as it is.

## started
Change the board as you like. Tap an empty point to place a stone; tap the stone at the top of the board to switch between Black and White. To remove a stone, tap it once to select it and tap it again to remove it; tapping anywhere else deselects it. Tap Done to play on from this position, or Cancel to go back to your game.

## status
{stone} Placing {colour} (tap to switch)

## done-button
Done

## done
Your position is set. {colour} to play.

## removed
I took off {count} stone(s) that had no liberties.

## cancelled
Set-up cancelled. Back to your game as it was.

## already
You are already setting up the board. Tap Done or Cancel when you have finished.

## finish-first
Finish what is on the board first: say "back to game", then "set up".
