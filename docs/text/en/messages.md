# Messages

The envelope at the top right: messages to Go Coach and the replies, with the number of replies waiting to be opened.

How to edit: change the words under a "## name" heading, but leave the heading itself as it is.
Words in curly brackets, such as {count}, are filled in by the app: keep them.

## button
Messages

## button-unread
Messages: {count} new

## title
Messages

## intro
Ask a question or tell us about a problem. Replies come within a day and appear here.

## loading
Loading…

## empty
No messages yet.

## list-failed
Could not load your messages. Check your connection and try again.

You
