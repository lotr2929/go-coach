# Go Coach grade

The player's Go Coach grade (grade.js): shown at the top beside the name, and in the chat after each game or lesson exercise
that counts. {whole} is the steady grade, {est} the precise estimate that moves after every result. {grade} is the whole text,
e.g. "18 kyu (17.62)"; {move} is "up 0.38", "down 0.12" or "unchanged"; {n} is a number.

How to edit: change the words under a "## name" heading, but leave the heading itself as it is.

## kyu
{whole} kyu ({est})

## dan
{whole} dan ({est})

## about
Your Go Coach grade: Go Coach's own ranking, built from your games and lessons. The first number is your steady grade; the one in brackets moves after every game and lesson. We try to keep it in line with international standards but cannot guarantee it.

## show
Show my Go Coach grade at the top

## up
up {n}

## down
down {n}

## same
unchanged

## after-game
Your Go Coach grade: {grade}, {move}.

## not-counted-help
This game does not count towards your Go Coach grade, as you had help (Coach mode, Analyse, a hint or undo). Play without help to build your grade.

## after-lesson
Right first time. Your Go Coach grade: {grade}, {move}.

## after-lesson-wrong
Your Go Coach grade: {grade}, {move}. Only your first try at each exercise counts, so take your time.

## promoted
Well done: you have gone up to {grade}!

## demoted
Your steady grade is now {grade}. A few good games will bring it back.
