# The Computer panel

The Computer panel (computer.js): with the board full screen in your own game, it shows what the computer does. {n} is the move number; {point} is a point such as E5.

How to edit: change the words under a "## name" heading, but leave the heading itself as it is.

## move
Move {n}: {point}

## pass
Move {n}: Pass

## thinking
Thinking…

## resigned
Resigns
