# Saving games

The Save button, and the Saved games tab of the New game box.

How to edit: change the words under a "## name" heading, but leave the heading itself as it is.
Words in curly brackets, such as {name}, are filled in by the app: keep them.

## saved
Game saved.

## save-failed
Could not save the game. Check your connection and try again.

## no-game
There is no game to save yet.

## loading
Loading your saved games...

## empty
No saved games yet. Press Save to keep the game you are playing.

## list-failed
Could not load your saved games. Check your connection and try again.

## meta
{size}×{size}, {moves} moves

## meta-one
{size}×{size}, 1 move

## rename
Rename

## delete
Delete

## name-aria
Name of the saved game

## load-confirm
Open this saved game? The game you are playing now will be replaced. Press Save first if you want to keep it.

## load-failed
Could not open that saved game. Check your connection and try again.

## opened
Saved game opened.

## delete-confirm
Delete "{name}" from your saved games?

## action-failed
That did not work. Check your connection and try again.
