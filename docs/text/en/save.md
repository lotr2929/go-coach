# Saving games

The Save button, and the Saved games tab of the New game box. Saved games are kept on the device only.

How to edit: change the words under a "## name" heading, but leave the heading itself as it is.
Words in curly brackets, such as {name}, are filled in by the app: keep them.

## saved
Game saved on this device.

## save-full
Could not save: this device's storage for Go Coach is full. Export or delete some saved games, then try again.

## no-game
There is no game to save yet.

## empty
No saved games yet. Press Save to keep the game you are playing.

## meta
{size}×{size}, {moves} moves

## meta-one
{size}×{size}, 1 move

## rename
Rename

## export
Export

## delete
Delete

## name-aria
Name of the saved game

## load-confirm
Open this saved game? The game you are playing now will be replaced. Press Save first if you want to keep it.

## opened
Saved game opened.

## opened-part
Saved game opened, but some of its moves could not be played on this board.

## delete-confirm
Delete "{name}" from your saved games?

## exported
Exported as {file}. Look for it in your downloads.

## import-failed
That file is not a Go game record (SGF) that Go Coach can open. Go Coach opens boards from 5×5 to 19×19.

## imported
Imported "{name}". Tap it to open it.

## imported-part
Imported "{name}" with its first {moves} moves; the rest could not be played on this board. Tap it to open it.
