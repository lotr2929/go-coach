## how-to
Use ‹ Back and Next ›, the slider or the arrow keys to step through the game. Tap any empty point to try your own move and see how the game might have continued. Ask me to "explain" a move or to "analyse" the position, and switch on the Territory tab under the board to see who is ahead. Say "exit" when you have finished.

## tap-letter
Tap a lettered point to see how it could continue. The percentages come from quick random games, so gaps under 5% mean about the same.

## territory
Switch on the Territory tab under the board to see who is likely to own each point at this moment of the game. Hover over a point to see its score, from 0 to 1.
