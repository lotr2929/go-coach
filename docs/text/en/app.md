# The app around the board

Small messages from all over the app: colour names, short notices (toasts), Settings, the update check, button tips and the reading page.

How to edit: change the words under a "## name" heading, but leave the heading itself as it is.
Words in curly brackets, such as {count}, are filled in by the app: keep them.

## count-white
{count} White stones

## count-black
{count} Black stones

## black
Black

## white

## reason.ko

## reason.suicide
