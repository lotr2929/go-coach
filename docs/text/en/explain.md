# Why one move beats another

The evidence the coach gives in "Why A beats B": both moves are played out many times and compared.
{a} is the better move and {b} the weaker one. The names of board areas come from commentary.md (region.…),
and the closing Go saying from proverbs.md.

How to edit: change the words under a "## name" heading, but leave the heading itself as it is.
Words in curly brackets are filled in by the app: keep them.

## points-better
I played each move out to the end ({games} quick games in all). On average {a} finishes about {points} points better for {who} than {b}.

## points-close
I played each move out to the end ({games} quick games in all). In points the two finish within one or two of each other, so the gap is small.

## points-worse
I played each move out to the end ({games} quick games in all). Here {b} actually scores about {points} points more on average, so the two are close and the ranking may be chance.

## survives

## keeps-alive

## kills

## lost
The stone at {b} itself is lost in {pct}% of the games: it does not have enough room or support to survive.
