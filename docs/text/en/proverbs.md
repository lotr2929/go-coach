# Go sayings

One saying sums up the evidence when the coach explains why one move beats another ("why A beats B").
"principle" and "proverb" are the labels; "sun" and "rules" are the sources. In each ".link", A is the better move and B the weaker one.

How to edit: change the words under a "## name" heading, but leave the heading itself as it is.
Words in curly brackets, such as {point}, are filled in by the app: keep them.

## sacrifice.text
Sacrifice stones to gain the initiative.

## sacrifice.link
A gives up a stone, but what it gains elsewhere is worth more.

## rescue.text
Don’t go fishing while your house is on fire.

## rescue.link
Saving your own group comes before taking points elsewhere: B leaves it to die.

## kill.text
In war, the way is to avoid what is strong and to strike at what is weak.

## kill.link
The weak group is the target: A catches it, B lets it get away.

## lost.text
Enter the opponent’s sphere gently.

## lost.link
A stone played too deep into the opponent’s area has no room to live, so B only feeds the opponent.

## punished.text
Be careful; do not move rashly.

## punished.link
B leaves a cutting point the opponent can use straight away.

## whole.text
The best thing of all is to take the enemy’s country whole and intact; to shatter and destroy it is not so good.

## whole.link
A gains more by building than B gains by attacking.

## big.text
Give up the small to take the large.

## big.link
Both moves have a point, but the area A claims is worth more.
