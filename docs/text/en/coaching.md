# Coaching: analysis, move feedback and game review

What the coach writes for Analyse, after each of your moves in Coach mode, for a game review, and when you switch between Play mode and Coach mode.
Headings appear in bold with a colon after them. The sections "Where things stand", "Safety" and "Biggest open area" take their
headings from commentary.md (the words before the colon there).

How to edit: change the words under a "## name" heading, but leave the heading itself as it is.
Words in curly brackets, such as {point}, are filled in by the app: keep them.

## coach-mode
Coach mode: after every turn I will suggest moves, and I will tell you how each of your moves went. Switch back to Play mode if you would rather play without help.

## play-mode
Play mode: I stay quiet unless you ask. Ask me for a hint, an analysis or a review at any time.
