# Go Coach is in beta

Go Coach is new, and "beta" means it is still being tried out. Thank you for being one of the first to use it.

## What this means for you

- **It is free.** Everything in Go Coach is free to use during the beta.
- **Things will change.** New lessons and features are added often, and some may look or work differently from one week to the next.
- **There may be mistakes.** If something looks wrong, or the coach says something odd, please tell us.

## Keeping your progress

Go Coach opens fresh every time, as if for the first time. If you do not sign in, nothing you do is kept after you close the app: your grade, games and chat start again next time.

To keep your progress, tap **Sign in** at the top and type a name. Your grade, streak, games and chat are kept under that name on this device and come back whenever you sign in with it again. For now your progress stays on this one device, and there is no password, so choose a name others on the device will not use.

## Telling us what you think

Tap the envelope at the top to send a message. We read every one, and your ideas decide what comes next.

## What we record

While you use Go Coach it sends a usage log: what you tap and type in the app, the positions on the board, how long you use it, and your screen size and browser. It also keeps an anonymous number for your device, so we can count how many people use Go Coach and how often they come back. The log does not include your name or email address. We use it only to improve Go Coach and never sell it. You can switch it off at any time in Settings ("Share usage log with Claude").

## After the beta

We plan to start subscriptions in the new year. Lessons, games against the computer and a Go Coach grade up to 10 kyu will stay free. A subscription will add a grade stronger than 10 kyu and more. We will tell you before anything changes.
