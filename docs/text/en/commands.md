# Text commands

What the coach shows when someone types "commands": how the chat box works, then the most useful things to type.
Lines that start and end with ** show as bold headings in the chat. Keep each example in quotation marks, so people can see exactly what to type.
The words the coach recognises are in the code; if you change an example, it must still be something the coach understands.

How to edit: change the words under a "## name" heading, but leave the heading itself as it is.

## list
**How the chat box works**
Type in the box at the bottom and press Send (or Enter). I understand short requests in plain English, a point on the board such as "D4", or a letter from my suggestions such as "A". I am not an AI model, so short, simple requests work best. If I do not understand, try the words shown below.

**During your game**
"hint": I suggest a good move and ring it on the board.
"why": the idea behind the last move I explained.
"analyse": my five best moves, marked A to E, with their chances of winning.
"why A?" or "compare A and B": what one suggestion does, or why one is better.
"what about D4?": what a move at D4 would do.
"play D4": play a move without tapping the board.
"am I in danger?": groups that are short of liberties.
"explain the position": who has which part of the board, and who is ahead.
"review": how each side played, move by move.
"undo", "pass", "resign", "new game".

**Looking ahead**
"show me": I pick the move I would play and follow it on a copy of the board. You step through it with "next" and "back" (or ← Back and Next → under the board), and can ask "why?" or other questions along the way.
"play both sides": on the other side's turn I mark my five best moves and you choose one, or play any other point. "computer plays" switches it off. You can also choose it in the New game box.
"explore D4" or "explore A": follow a move a few steps ahead on a copy of the board. Then "next", "back", "another" (a different reply) and "back to game".

**Learning**
"teach me", "lessons" or "lesson 3": short lessons with a task each.
"basic rules": step-by-step teaching on a 9×9 board.
"guided game": a first game with the coach explaining each move.
"set up the board": place and remove stones by hand (also the Set up button), then play on from that position.
"what is ko?" or "what does kifu mean?": the meaning of a Go term (also liberty, atari, eye, joseki, kyu and dan, hane, tsumego, moyo, ponnuki, tiger's mouth and many more).
"rules", "first move", "checklist": the rules in brief, where to start, and five questions to ask before every move.
"famous games" and "history of Go".

**Settings**
"show liberties" or "hide liberties", "show coordinates" or "hide coordinates", "turn on sound" or "turn off sound".

Type "help" to see the welcome again, or "commands" to see this list.
