# Running a lesson

What the coach says around the lessons in lessons.md: opening a lesson, answering a quiz, resetting, moving on.
Words in curly brackets, such as {count}, are filled in by the app: keep them.

How to edit: change the words under a "## name" heading, but leave the heading itself as it is.

## menu-title
The lessons

## heading
Lesson {count} of {total}: {title}

## heading-chapter
Lesson {count} of {total} ({chapter}): {title}

## chapter-head
Chapter {count}: {title}

## task
Your task: {task}

## ask-me
Ask me if anything is unclear, or say "back to game" to return to your game.

## play-a-game
Play a game

## next
Next

## correct
Correct. {explain}

## not-quite
Not quite. Have another look.

## reset
Lesson reset. Have another go.

## ready
Ready for your first game? I will set it up for you and explain every move.
