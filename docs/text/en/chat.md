# The chat

Short replies when a request typed in the chat cannot be done right now, and the chat's own buttons.

How to edit: change the words under a "## name" heading, but leave the heading itself as it is.

## cannot
I cannot do that right now.

## not-your-turn
It is not your turn.

## hints-on-turn
Hints are available on your turn.

## nothing-to-explain
There is nothing to explain yet. Ask for a hint, or wait for the computer’s move.

## no-game
There is no game yet.

## in-game
You are already in your game.

## no-ring
There is no move to play in this lesson, so there is no blue ring.

## finish-first
Finish the task first, then press Next.

## busy
We are already in the middle of something. Say "back to game" to leave it first.

## game-only
That works in the game. Say "back to game" first.

## shrink
Shrink the chat

## enlarge
Enlarge the chat

## history-hint
Pull down to see earlier sessions

## notes
Coach's notes

## welcome-head
Welcome
