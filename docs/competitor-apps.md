# Competitor apps

What we know about other Go apps, so any session can compare Go Coach with them. Add an app as its own section. Each fact comes from the sources listed under it; check prices again before quoting them, as they change and differ by country.

## SmartGo (closest competitor)

Researched 25 November 2026 from App Store listings and SmartGo's own pages. No independent reviews or user numbers were found.

**Who makes it.** Anders Kierulf, a computer-Go specialist (PhD in computer science, ETH Zürich, 1990), who has worked on SmartGo full-time since 1999. One experienced person, not a company.

**Platforms.** iPhone, iPad and Mac only (SmartGo One). No Android or web version was found.

**Engine.** KataGo, a strong neural-network engine, used for hints, move analysis and play. Since December 2024 it can analyse a whole game and mark every bad move.

**Content.**
- About 120,000 professional game records.
- About 150 Go books with diagrams you can play through.
- A tutorial with over 100 problems.
- A search that compares your game with professional games.
- Recording, annotating and editing your own games, with a move tree that keeps variations (the idea behind Go Coach's move tree, added 25 Nov 2026).
- Board sizes 9×9, 11×11, 13×13 and 19×19; manual or automatic handicap; territory estimate; hints; undo and replay.
- An older iPad-only app, Go Books, last updated in 2020.

**Price** (varies by country):
- Free to download; advanced features need Pro.
- Pro: about US$3–5 a month, or a one-off lifetime unlock of about US$75–119 (A$119 in Australia).
- Books and collections sold separately, for example "Pro Games and Features" at US$19.99.

**Who it is for.** Players who already know the game: records, notes, books and pro games. Beginners mostly get the problems and the books.

**How Go Coach differs.**
- Coaching in plain words: SmartGo shows good and bad moves; Go Coach explains why, plays the evidence out and ends with a fitting saying. Nothing like this was found in SmartGo.
- A start for complete beginners: the guided first game, step-by-step lessons and Shapes marked on your own board. SmartGo starts higher up.
- Reach and price: Go Coach runs in any browser, Android included, and is free. SmartGo is Apple-only and paid for the full version.
- Where SmartGo is ahead: engine strength (a neural network; World of Go's job, not Go Coach's), its library of books and pro games, and whole-game review.
