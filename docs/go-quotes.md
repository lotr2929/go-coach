# Go quotes: a working list

A collection of quotes about Go for possible use in the app, with how well each one is sourced. This is a reference file, not app wording: anything chosen for the app goes into docs/text/en (see proverbs.md) and follows the rule in docs/decisions.md that quotes must be real and attributed.

Started 24 November 2026 from two web pages:
- Wikiquote, "Go (game)": https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Go_(game) (lists only sourced quotes on the page; unsourced ones are on its talk page)
- Sensei's Library, "Great Quotes": https://senseis.xmp.net/?GreatQuotes (a wiki anyone can edit; many quotes have no source)

Status words used below:
- **Checked**: read in a book Boon supplied.
- **Sourced**: the web page gives a book, article or event, but nobody has checked it yet.
- **Attributed**: no source, or the source is disputed. Show as "attributed to" if used.

## Checked against Boon's books
(From the 24 Nov 2026 session; see docs/phone-notes. Not yet used in the app.)

- "One cannot win a game just because he wants to. The outside world is always disturbing. If my mind is shaken by this disturbance, I would lose." Go Seigen, in *Go on Go*.
- "It is only common sense to drive your enemy towards your thick positions and away from your thin ones." Kaoru Iwamoto, *Go for Beginners* (1972), p. 107.
- Yuan Zhou's summary of Go Seigen's view (not Go Seigen's own words): the essence of the game is fighting, playing Go is a search for the truth, and the primary concern is understanding rather than winning. *Master Play* (2008), p. 3.

## Sourced (from Wikiquote)

- "Go is hard." Cho U, after winning the last game of the 30th Meijin title match against Kobayashi Satoru, November 2005. Also translated as "Go is difficult".
- "Becoming one stone stronger is the supreme enjoyment." Go Seigen, *A Way of Play for the 21st Century* (2008).
- "Saying 'just one game' they began to play... That was yesterday." Senryū, translated by William Pinckard, *Go World* #15, p. 59.
- "Go is much more like the panorama of an entire campaign, or complex theatre of war." Oscar Korschelt, *The Theory and Practice of Go* (1880; English translation by King and Leckie, 1965), pp. 6-7.
- "The board must be square and represents the laws of the earth. The lines must be straight like the divine virtues." Ban Gu (32-92 AD), *Yi Zhi* (The Essence of Go).
- "When starting, the best strategy is to spread the pieces far apart and stretch them out..." Huan Tan (c. 43 BC-28 AD), *Xin Lun* (New Treatise). Wikiquote's text may continue; check the full quote before use.
- "The number of all things in Nature begins with one." *Wang You Qing Le Ji* (The Carefree and Innocent Pastime Collection), early 12th century, the oldest surviving Go manual; quoted in *Go in Ancient China*.
- "A game of go is like a spring whose waters go only where they can go and stop where they have to stop." Liang Weijin (1680-1760), quoted in John Fairbairn's essay on Fan Xiping and Shi Ding'an (11 January 2000).
- "I, who am ignorant of so many things, know that I am ignorant of one more..." Jorge Luis Borges, poem "El Go", translated by Wayne Nelson. A modern translation: short extract only.

## Sourced (from Sensei's Library)

- "The tactic of the soldier, the exactness of the mathematician, the imagination of the artist, the inspiration of the poet, the calm of the philosopher, and the greatest intelligence." Zhang Yunqi on the qualities needed to excel at weiqi, *Weiqi de faxian* (Discovering weiqi), Chinese Weiqi Institute, 1991, p. 2.
- "The subtleties of this game are beyond the reach of the lazy, its triumph is too exquisite for the vulgar and materialistic man..." Herbert A. Giles, "Wei-chi, or the Chinese game of war", *Temple Bar*, vol. 49, 1877, p. 45.
- "The aim of go is relative advantage; the game is played all over the board, and the objective is to increase one's options and reduce those of the adversary. The goal is less victory than persistent strategic progress." Henry Kissinger, quoted in *Newsweek*, 8 November 2004.
- "Go is science, art and game..." Kajiwara Takeo, interviewed at the European Go Congress, Budapest, 1986.
- "When a monkey falls out of a tree, it is still a monkey. But when a meijin loses his title, he becomes just another go player." Nakayama Noriyuki, *The Treasure Chest Enigma*.
- "A nation which plays this game is to be reckoned with!" An unnamed Chicago company executive, quoted in Edward Lasker's *Go and Go-Moku* (c. 1934). Boon has this book: worth checking.
- "To answer without thinking leads to many defeats." Shi Ding'an (no source given beyond the name).

## Attributed only (no source, or disputed)

- "The rules of go are so elegant, organic, and rigorously logical that if intelligent life forms exist elsewhere in the universe, they almost certainly play go." Edward Lasker, often also given to Emanuel Lasker. Sensei's editors could not agree on a source; check *Go and Go-Moku* and Lasker's *Chess for Fun and Chess for Blood*.
- "The board is a mirror of the mind of the players as the moments pass..." "Moritz". Unsourced on both Wikiquote and Sensei's Library.
- "Go uses the most elemental materials and concepts -- line and circle, wood and stone, black and white -- combining them with simple rules to generate subtle strategies and complex tactics that stagger the imagination." Iwamoto Kaoru. No source given; *Go for Beginners* is worth checking.
- "Go is not about winning through brilliant moves, it is about losing through bad moves." Sakata Eio. Only source given is a forum post.
- "Studying go is a wonderful way to develop both the creative as well as the logical abilities of children..." Cho Chikun. No source given.
- "A few moments to learn, a lifetime to master." Called an ancient proverb; probably a modern saying.
- "Go is to chess what philosophy is to double-entry accounting." From the novel *Shibumi* by Trevanian: a character's line, not a fact.

## Not real quotes or wrongly attributed
- "Go is about the order of moves" is a Japanese proverb (碁は手順なり), not Rin Kaiho's own words.
- "By playing Go we become good friends" (Takagawa Shukaku) is most likely the old Chinese saying "Use Go to meet friends" (以棋会友).

## Left out on purpose
- Lines from *Hikaru no Go* (Hotta Yumi, Fujiwara no Sai): copyrighted manga characters.
- Sensei's "Misc Tag Lines" by wiki users: jokes, not quotes from notable people.
